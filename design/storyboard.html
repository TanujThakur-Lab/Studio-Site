OGC Studio · storyboard 1

One scroll, one person, one week. The page is a 3D space you fall through from top to bottom. At the top a person is buried in messages, tasks and alerts. Each scroll step hands a piece of that work to OGC, and the scene gets quieter, slower and lighter until there is open time and a calm horizon. The thing we sell is time back.

These are stills of six scroll positions. Between them everything moves continuously: the camera travels down through the scene, pieces drift and change, colour shifts from night to morning.

How the scroll feels as a whole

7:58 am · chaoshand-offapproveafternoon · calm
  • Pace slows as you go down. Near the top, a small scroll changes a lot. Near the bottom, the same scroll changes very little, so the page itself feels calmer.
  • One constant layer: the OGC Studio name, the theme switch and “Book a call” stay put while everything else transforms. That keeps it usable and minimal.
  • The theme switch (◐) is a placeholder here; it is the next page we design (for example “Calm”, “Frontier”, “Paper”).
  • 3D build: Three.js (WebGL) with a liquid shader for frame 3. On phones and with reduced motion, the six frames show as still panels with gentle fades, so everything still reads.
  • Honesty rules stay: demo content is labelled as sample data, and “hours back” stays a placeholder until there is a real case.
