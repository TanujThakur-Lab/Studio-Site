## Scope

`src/pages/index.astro`: the single-page marketing site. Mode: **Persuade**. Built code-first (no image generation available).

## Audience and action

The audience is owners and managers of service businesses (shipping, freight, IT, professional offices). The one action is to book a free 20-minute call. There is no real proof yet: every proof slot is a visible placeholder, and every demo uses labelled sample data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is laid out as the finished, edited weekly client report the studio's workflows produce. It refuses the AI-agency default of a centred hero over a gradient, icon cards, logos and three pricing cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- Paper `#F0F0ED`, ink `#131417` and one approval chartreuse `#C8F03C`, used as fills only. In dark mode the ground is `#111214` and the ink `#E6E6E3`. (Amended 2026-10-22, pending the owner's sign-off: both neutrals moved slightly off yellow-cream after the Taste palette check. The user-approved values were `#F2F1EC` and `#ECEBE5`.)
- Archivo sets every structural element (heavy and tight for titles). Newsreader appears only inside finished documents.
- Documents are paper-white sheets, built in HTML at true size.
- Colour pages are whole sections in flat ink or chartreuse. There are no lines, shadows or gradients. A 42% column axis organises the text.
- Photography: one archival photograph (Head office typists’ room, NZ Railways, 1959; CC BY-SA 2.0, credited in the footer), in greyscale, in the problem mosaic. It shows the history of copy-paste admin and is never presented as the studio’s own work. (Added 2026-10-22.)
- The tiers are set as a proposal sheet, not as cards.

STORY: Fridays go to copy-paste admin. We build a workflow that drafts the work, and a person approves it. The visitor sees four of these, sees that this page is the proof, picks a tier, has their objections answered, and books the call.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The top 50% is a full-width desk band. 11 messy ticket notes (amended from about 14 so the report fits the band) lean towards the cursor, then fly into a serif weekly report sheet on the right. The report waits for the visitor to press "Approve", and the tick lands.
- Below the band sits the h1 "Get your Fridays back." at about 7vw, starting at the left margin. (Amended 2026-10-22: the Taste inspector measured a 600×660px void in the left half while the h1 sat on the axis, so the h1 now spans from the margin and the standfirst and button hold the axis.)
- The standfirst and the magnetic chartreuse "Book a free 20-minute call" button sit under the h1. The nav carries the wordmark and the button.

FORM: "The printed annual" (the Taste skill's editorial-report composition, chosen from the three compositions Taste's own draw dealt with palette and type kept). Signature interaction: the hero's mess-to-report assembly with a human Approve. Motion grammar: springs (snappy 420/32, smooth 220/28, gentle 120/22), a 60 or 90ms stagger, transform and opacity only, and scroll-scrubbed stages. seed: none (user-pinned). The user approved the plan on 2026-10-22, including the concept "Nothing leaves without a tick", the sign-off-desk world, the Archivo/Newsreader type and the paper/ink/chartreuse palette, before any Impeccable roll, so no concept-seed roll was run.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The visitor presses Approve on a report that assembled itself out of a mess of notes.
