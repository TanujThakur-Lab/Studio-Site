# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) plus the Motion library for spring and scroll animation, chosen by Claude and approved in the plan (2026-10-22). Astro ships zero JS by default so only the hero scene, scroll scenes and demos carry scripts; it is Vite underneath. Target host: Cloudflare Pages (kept local until the owner decides to publish).

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized service businesses: shipping agencies, freight forwarders, IT and consulting firms, professional offices. They lose hours every week to repetitive admin (weekly client reports, ticket follow-ups, document updates, routine replies) and fear that AI will send something wrong to a client.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for [BRAND NAME], a two-person studio that builds Claude-powered admin workflows. Success: within ten seconds a visitor thinks "I want something like this for my business", and books a free 20-minute call. The page itself is the proof of the studio's quality.

## Positioning

Repetitive admin becomes a reliable AI workflow that a person approves before anything reaches a client. Small, fixed-scope, run and improved monthly. Talk about hours saved and mistakes avoided, never about AI itself.

## Operating Context

- Based in India; serves Indian and international firms. British English spelling.
- Visitors arrive from a shared link, WhatsApp or search, often on a phone.
- Offer ladder: (1) a small paid workflow audit, (2) a fixed-price build, (3) a monthly plan where the studio runs and improves the workflow. No prices are set: show "Pricing on request".
- Contact: booking link, WhatsApp and email, all placeholders until supplied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All on-page demos are fully scripted with sample data; no real AI calls, no API keys, no trackers or analytics, no third-party requests (fonts self-hosted).
- Brand name is the placeholder `[BRAND NAME]`.
- No founders section for now (owner's decision, 2026-10-22).

## Brand Commitments

Voice: confident, plain-spoken, practical. No buzzwords, no "revolutionize", no hype about AI.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No clients, testimonials, logos, case studies, awards or statistics may be shown or invented. Proof slots are visible placeholders such as "[Your first case study goes here]". All demo data is labelled as sample data.

## Product Principles

1. Human approval is the product: nothing goes to a client without a person's sign-off.
2. Show the workflow working, don't describe AI.
3. Honest scale: a two-person studio, no inflated claims.
4. One action: book the free 20-minute call.
5. The site is the demo: its own craft is the proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast in light and dark mode, full reduced-motion version that still communicates every workflow.
